Overview

Alila Mayakoba · 4 Nights

All four of us take red-eyes on 10/29, meet at CUN airport early on 10/30, and ride to the hotel together. Mostly resort time, with one ruins day and one water day: a private day trip to Chichén Itzá, a cenote and Valladolid, and reef snorkeling in Puerto Morelos. The last day stays in: spa, then the airport. Two picks from Mayakoba's Día de Muertos festival: the canal boat show and the Aquí Me Quedo cookout dinner.
